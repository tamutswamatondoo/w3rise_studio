Est. 2019 · Geneva

Twenty-six people, four disciplines, one standard.

w3rise Studio began as a two-person practice with a stubborn belief: that the quality of the idea should survive all the way to production. Seven years on we're a team of strategists, designers, engineers and creative technologists who refuse the usual hand-offs between them.

01 — Principles

Five rules we don't bend.

01

One team, start to finish

The people who scope the work are the people who deliver it. No account layer, no relay race, no translation loss between the idea and the build.

02

Design in the browser

Real components with real content, early. Static mockups hide the problems that matter — responsiveness, motion, state, performance.

03

Performance is a feature

Every engagement ships against a measurable performance and accessibility budget. If it isn't fast and usable, it isn't finished.

04

Craft over volume

We take on a limited number of engagements each year so each one gets the senior attention it needs. Small by design, not by accident.

05

Leave it documented

Systems, tokens and decisions are handed over in writing. The goal is a client team that can run the work without us — and still call us anyway.

02 — Story

From two desks to three cities.

2019

Two people and a shared studio desk

Founded in Geneva around a simple offer: brand and build, from the same small team.

2021

First enterprise platform

A financial data terminal took the studio from marketing sites into production software — and set the direction for everything after.

2022

Munich opens, interactive lab launches

A dedicated practice for real-time 3D and physical installation work, staffed by creative technologists.

2024

New York, and the hundredth launch

Our first US studio, opened to serve institutional and frontier-technology clients closer to their teams.

2026

26 specialists, one continuous standard

Still deliberately small, still senior-led, still insisting that the four disciplines sit in the same room.
